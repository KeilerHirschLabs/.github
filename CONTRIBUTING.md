# Contributing to KeilerHirsch Labs projects

We welcome useful, focused contributions to public repositories **when that project accepts outside contributions**.

1. Read the target repository's README, license, and any local contributing policy first; local project guidance takes precedence.
2. For substantial or potentially disruptive work, open an issue or discuss scope before implementing.
3. Explain what your change does, how you verified it, and which limitations remain. Keep pull requests reviewable.
4. Do not include credentials, private conversations, player/customer data, protected material, or third-party code/assets without an appropriate right to contribute them.
5. Identify important external sources, licensing obligations, and material AI assistance. Do not claim tests or integrations you did not actually verify.
6. Contributions must be compatible with the **target project's** licensing and contribution terms. If the rights are unclear, clarify **before** submitting code.

There is no organization-wide CLA or DCO requirement. This does **not** grant rights to proprietary projects or authorize reuse of private Labs research. Maintainers may decline or request changes to contributions.
