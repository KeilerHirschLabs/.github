# Community conduct

KeilerHirsch Labs welcomes direct, constructive technical disagreement. Critique ideas, code, and evidence, not people.

- Be respectful and precise, including when a design or claim is disputed.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, publish private information, or target people with abuse.
- Keep bug reports, pull requests, and discussions relevant to the project.
- Do not post malicious links or spam.

Maintainers may moderate, close, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. For abuse on GitHub, use the platform's reporting controls; do not publish private allegations or sensitive evidence in public issues.
